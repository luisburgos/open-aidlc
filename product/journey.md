# Journey

The stages a person goes through with the product, named by what they do rather than by screen. Screens change; stages hold. A brief names the stages its bet covers, and leaves the rest alone.

## How a stage is written

A heading that names what the person does, in the words of [`domain.md`](domain.md). A sentence or two of what happens in it, including what happens today without the product, where that is where things go wrong.

**Today:** what in the product serves this stage now, as links to the flows of the PRDs in [`prds/`](prds/). A stage the product does not serve yet says so; that is where a bet may be.

## The moment that matters

One point of the journey, where the product wins or loses its place in the person's life, said once and in a sentence. A bet that moves it outranks one that polishes a stage around it.
