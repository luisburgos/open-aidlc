# Principles

Decision filters. Each one rejects something tempting, or it would not be here.

When a proposed feature conflicts with one of these, the principle wins or the principle changes. Quietly making an exception is how a principle stops meaning anything.

## How a principle is written

A heading that states the position. A sentence or two of what it means in practice. Then:

**Rejects:** the specific things it rules out. Name features, not qualities. A principle that rejects nothing concrete is a value, and values filter nothing.

**Cost, stated plainly:** what is lost by holding it, where the loss is real. A principle with no cost was not a decision.

Both lines are optional in form and mandatory in substance. If neither can be written, the principle is not one yet.

## What is not a principle

A quality everyone wants. Simplicity, quality, speed, delight: each rejects nothing on its own, so each filters nothing. The test is whether a reasonable person could propose the opposite. If not, it is a value, and it belongs in the vision rather than here.
